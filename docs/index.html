Everything you need to present a prototype

  • Auto-zoom on clicks

    ScreenStudio records every click and generates smooth zooms that follow your cursor. Fine-tune timing, scale and easing on the timeline.

  • Capture what matters

    Record a full screen, a custom area or a single window, with your microphone, system audio and camera. Pause and resume any time.

  • Camera layouts

    Show yourself as a bubble, side by side with the screen, or full frame. Choose the shape, size, border and position.

  • Polished by default

    Backgrounds, padding, rounded corners, shadows, cursor highlights and click ripples make a plain recording look designed.

  • Export for anywhere

    One-click presets for YouTube, Shorts and TikTok, LinkedIn and presentations, or choose format, resolution, frame rate and quality yourself. MP4 and GIF.

  • Private by design

    Everything stays on your computer. No account, no uploads, no tracking. Your recordings are plain files in your Documents folder.

From idea to share-ready in three steps

  1. 1

    Record

    Pick what to capture, check the summary, press Start. A small bar keeps out of the way while you work.

  2. 2

    Edit

    Your zooms are already placed. Select anything on the timeline or canvas to adjust just that.

  3. 3

    Export

    Choose a preset and export. Rendering runs once and the file is encoded as it goes.
